P0 checklist for team packs (Bronze+). Not legal advice. Full text: docs/installation/OPERATOR-TRUST-RUNBOOK.md

Before HR

  1. Prefer Copper if you do not store employee personal data.
  2. Complete privacy acknowledgement in setup when enabling people data.
  3. Change default admin password; limit admins.
  4. Plan database backups.
